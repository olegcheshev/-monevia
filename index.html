Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a wallet?

Open the Wallets tab and tap the add button. Enter the wallet name, currency, and initial balance.

How do I record an expense?

Open a wallet, tap the add transaction button, select Expense, enter the amount, and choose a category.

Can I use multiple currencies?

Yes. You can create wallets in different currencies and manage them separately.

How do I back up my data?

Open Settings and use the backup feature to save a copy of your financial data.
