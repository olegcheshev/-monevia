Monevia — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

1. Overview

Monevia is a personal finance management application. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

2. Financial Data

Financial information entered into Monevia is stored locally on your device. We do not operate servers that collect or store your financial transactions.

3. Data Collection

Monevia does not require account registration. We do not collect financial transaction data or use it for advertising.

4. Backups

You can create and restore backups of your data. You control where backup files are stored and shared. If you save backups to a cloud storage provider, that provider's privacy policy applies.

5. Third-Party Services

Monevia does not include third-party advertising or analytics services.

6. Data Security

Your financial data remains on your device unless you choose to export or back it up. We recommend protecting your device with a passcode or biometric authentication.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary. Changes will be published on this page.
